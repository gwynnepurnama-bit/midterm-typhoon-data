Key insights
  • Lower pressure means stronger wind: every storm below 910 hPa reached at least 105 knots (Tip 1979: 870 hPa and 165 knots).
  • Wind is not the whole story: Morakot (2009) had the weakest wind here (65 knots) but caused deadly floods in Taiwan with record rain.
  • Typhoons build up over days: Champi (2026) grew from 23 to 117 knots in about a week.
Data: Central Weather Administration (CWA), Taiwan, Open Data (live typhoons and Champi 2026). Other storms: sample data based on approximate peak values from Japan Meteorological Agency (JMA) records.
